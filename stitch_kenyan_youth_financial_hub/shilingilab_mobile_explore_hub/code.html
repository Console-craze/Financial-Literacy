Decode Adult Talk

Quick street translation of financial jargon used at Sunday dinner.

Okoa Jahazi is prepaid airtime credit that cuts 10% advance fee instantly. Fuliza is a continuous line of bank overdraft credit with daily maintenance tariffs until your next M-PESA deposit.

A merry-go-round only recirculates cash for instant household needs (zero compounding). An investment chama buys assets (land, Money Market Funds, treasury bills) to produce quarterly profit dividends.

Sending KES 100 has zero transaction fee to other M-PESA users. Sending KES 101 pushes you directly into Tier 2 (KES 7 fee). Always watch your single-shilling borders!

When banks won't take your high school ID, your word and your parents' neighborhood reputation act as the guarantor for micro-loans. High trust = low friction; default = community isolation.
