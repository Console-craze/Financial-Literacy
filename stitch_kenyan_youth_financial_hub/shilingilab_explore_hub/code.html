Fuliza vs Okoa Jahazi

Short answer: One is an M-PESA cash overdraft, the other is an airtime loan with a 10% fee upfront.

Fuliza lets you complete a transaction when balance is zero. Okoa gives KES 100 airtime but only KES 90 reaches your balance immediately.

Chama Model cached

Merry-Go-Round vs Investment

Short answer: Cash swapping vs asset buying.

A merry-go-round just redistributes money with zero interest. An investment chama pools capital to purchase real estate or money market funds.

Tariff Tiers & Excise Duty

Short answer: The price jumps when you cross thresholds like KES 500 or 1,000.

Kenya applies an excise duty tax on money transfer fees. Crossing from KES 499 to KES 501 bumps you into the next, steeper bracket.

Trust Ledger handshake

Social Collateral

Short answer: Why your aunt needed two co-signers to get a loan.

Without title deeds or logbooks, Kenyan microfinance uses group liability. If she defaults, her closest friends pay. That is social collateral.
