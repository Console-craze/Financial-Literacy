Street-Smart Mental Models

The High School Survival Playbook

3 Tested Heuristics
1

The 24-Hour Canteen Rule

Never buy tuckshop snacks during the first 15 minutes after the sports or prep bell when appetite peaks. Wait 24 hours. If you still crave the samosa after dining hall supper, purchase half. 80% of canteen cravings vanish after a cup of hot water.

verified Saves ~KES 900 / term
2

Split Supplies from Pleasure

Upon stepping onto school grounds, immediately tuck away KES 800 into your Mathematical Set tin or dictionary for printing, exam clipboards, and extra pens. Once money mingles in your uniform pocket, your brain treats all of it as snack food.

verified Zero exam prep panic
3

The Unlendable KES 200 Buffer

Have a clear phrase memorized for friends asking to borrow emergency cash: "Niko na fare ya kwenda home pekee" (I only have my direct home bus fare). That final KES 200 note is non-negotiable insurance against walking 12km in heavy rain.

verified Protects transit safety
