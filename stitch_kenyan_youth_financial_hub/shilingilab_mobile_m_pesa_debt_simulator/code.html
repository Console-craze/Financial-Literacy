Pedagogical Deep Dives

visibility_off

1. The Psychology of Invisible Money

Cash gives you tactile friction: you feel the paper note leave your fingers. When a green prompt simply asks “Fuliza M-PESA? Yes/No”, your brain fails to register that you signed a high-interest legal micro-contract in 1.4 seconds.

lock_reset

2. The Zero-Balance Hijack Trap

Fuliza has immediate priority deduction. Any incoming funds (gift from uncle, refund, side hustle gig deposit) are swept instantly before you can access them. You lose liquidity control until the loan ledger reaches zero.

trending_up

3. The Tariff Threshold Cliff

Sending KES 500 costs KES 7, but sending KES 501 jumps into the next Safaricom band costing KES 13. Street-smart youth split or negotiate transactions right beneath band thresholds to preserve pocket margins.
