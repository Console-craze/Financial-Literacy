Street-smart mental models

The High School Survival Playbook

3 tested heuristics
1

The 24-Hour Canteen Rule

Never buy tuckshop snacks during the first 15 minutes after sports or prep bell, when appetite peaks. Wait. If you still crave a samosa after supper, buy half. Many cravings pass after a cup of hot water.

◎ Saves ~KES 900 / term
2

Split Supplies from Pleasure

Set aside KES 800 for printing, exam clipboards, and extra pens as soon as you receive pocket money. Keep essential school costs separate from snack money.

◎ Zero exam-prep panic
3

The Unlendable KES 200 Buffer

Keep a small emergency reserve for direct fare home or medical needs. Agree on a kind, clear phrase when friends ask to borrow it: “Niko na fare ya kwenda home pekee.”

◎ Protects transit safety
